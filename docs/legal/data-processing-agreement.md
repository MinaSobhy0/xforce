# Data Processing Agreement

**INTERNAL DOCUMENT — Not published. Send to customers on request.**

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") forms part of the Terms of Service between XForce HR ("Processor") and the subscribing Customer ("Controller") and takes effect on the date the Customer accepts the Terms of Service.

---

## 1. Definitions

- **Personal Data** — any information relating to an identified or identifiable individual processed by the Processor on behalf of the Controller.
- **Processing** — any operation performed on Personal Data, including collection, storage, retrieval, use, disclosure, or deletion.
- **Data Subject** — the individual to whom Personal Data relates (primarily the Controller's employees).
- **Applicable Law** — Egyptian Personal Data Protection Law No. 151 of 2020 and any other data protection law applicable to the Controller's use of the Service.

## 2. Subject matter and duration

The Processor processes Personal Data on behalf of the Controller solely to provide the XForce HR Service as described in the Terms of Service. This DPA remains in effect for the duration of the subscription and terminates when the Customer Data is deleted in accordance with §8.

## 3. Nature, purpose, and categories of data

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Purpose** | Providing the XForce HR platform: employee record management, attendance, leave, payroll, loans, and document storage |
| **Nature** | Storage, retrieval, display, export, and deletion as instructed by the Controller |
| **Categories of Personal Data** | Identity data (name, national ID), contact data (email, phone), employment data (job title, department, salary, attendance, leave, contracts), device data (IP address, device model) |
| **Categories of Data Subjects** | Controller's employees, contractors, and other individuals whose HR records the Controller uploads to the Service |

## 4. Controller's instructions

The Processor shall process Personal Data only on documented instructions from the Controller. The Controller's use of the Service (uploading data, configuring settings, triggering exports) constitutes documented instructions. If the Processor is required by law to process Personal Data in a manner not instructed by the Controller, the Processor will notify the Controller before processing unless prohibited by law.

## 5. Processor obligations

### 5.1 Confidentiality
The Processor ensures that personnel authorised to process Personal Data are bound by confidentiality obligations.

### 5.2 Security
The Processor implements and maintains appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect Personal Data against accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, or unauthorised disclosure. Current measures include TLS encryption in transit, encryption at rest, per-customer database schema isolation, role-based access control, and audit logging. Details are described in the Security section of the Privacy Policy.

### 5.3 Subprocessors
The Controller grants general authorisation for the Processor to engage subprocessors. Current subprocessors are listed in Annex A. The Processor will notify the Controller of any intended changes to subprocessors with at least 14 days' notice, giving the Controller the opportunity to object on reasonable data-protection grounds. If the Controller objects, the parties will negotiate in good faith to resolve the concern; if no resolution is reached within 30 days, the Controller may terminate the affected portion of the Service without penalty. The Processor remains liable for subprocessors' compliance with this DPA.

### 5.4 Data subject rights
The Processor will assist the Controller in responding to Data Subject requests (access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability) to the extent technically feasible, given the nature of the processing. The Controller is responsible for receiving and responding to requests from its employees.

### 5.5 Controller compliance assistance
The Processor will provide reasonable assistance to the Controller in fulfilling its obligations under Applicable Law, including data protection impact assessments and breach notifications, taking into account the nature of processing and the information available to the Processor.

### 5.6 Breach notification
The Processor will notify the Controller without undue delay — and in any event within 72 hours of becoming aware — of a Personal Data breach affecting the Controller's data. The notification will be sent to the Controller's registered administrator contact and will include: nature of the breach, categories and approximate number of Data Subjects affected, likely consequences, and measures taken or proposed to address the breach.

### 5.7 Audit rights
Upon the Controller's written request (no more than once per year unless a breach has occurred), the Processor will provide information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with this DPA, including any then-current third-party audit reports if available. The Controller may also conduct an audit at its own cost with at least 30 days' prior notice, subject to reasonable confidentiality restrictions.

## 6. International transfers

Some subprocessors listed in Annex A operate outside Egypt. The Processor ensures that any transfer of Personal Data outside Egypt is subject to standard contractual clauses or equivalent safeguards in accordance with Egyptian Law 151/2020 Art. 12.

## 7. Controller's obligations

The Controller warrants that:
- It has a lawful basis for processing the Personal Data it uploads to the Service.
- It has provided employees with appropriate privacy notices regarding processing in the Service.
- It has obtained any consents required by Applicable Law.
- Its instructions to the Processor comply with Applicable Law.

## 8. Deletion on termination

On termination of the subscription, the Processor will, at the Controller's choice, delete or return all Personal Data within 30 days, and delete existing copies unless Applicable Law requires retention. Where the Controller requests return, data is provided in a structured, machine-readable format (e.g. CSV or JSON). The Controller may request a structured data export during the 30-day export window described in the Terms of Service.

## 9. Liability

Each party's liability under this DPA is subject to the limitations set out in the Terms of Service.

## 10. Conflict of terms

In the event of a conflict between this DPA and the Terms of Service or any other agreement between the parties on matters of personal data protection, this DPA prevails.

## 11. Governing law

This DPA is governed by the laws of the Arab Republic of Egypt. Disputes are subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent courts in Cairo, Egypt.
